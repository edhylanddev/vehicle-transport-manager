Add it to your home screen (one time)

  1. In Safari, open {{ config.APP_URL.replace('https://', '') }}.
  2. Tap the Share button . It's at the bottom of the screen, or next to the address bar. If you don't see it, tap ⋯ first.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen . If you see an Open as Web App switch, leave it on.
  4. Tap Add. A VTM icon appears on your home screen.
  5. Open VTM from the home screen and sign in{% if config.ALLOW_SIGNUP %}, or tap Create account if you're new{% endif %}. You stay signed in.

Morning notification (owners and office staff)

In the VTM app, go to My account and turn on Sunrise report notification, then tap Allow. Every morning at 6:00 you'll get a summary of yesterday's business. Needs iOS 16.4 or newer.

Camera, photo uploads, signatures and location all work in the iPhone app. When the iPhone asks for the camera or location, tap Allow. Location is used to stamp where pickups and deliveries happen. Have an Android phone? Get the Android app.
